Register a certificate

Three ways to do this. Uploading the file is best, because the expiry date is then read from the certificate itself and cannot be mistyped.

Upload a certificate file

Accepted: .pfx, .p12, .pem, .crt, .cer, .der, .p7b, .p7c. Only the public certificate will be saved. The private key and the password stay on your computer — .pfx and .p12 files are opened by your browser, and the file itself is never sent anywhere.
